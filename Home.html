Welcome to QuestSafe

QuestSafe helps your kid connect with other young gamers in communities like Minecraft, Roblox, and Pokémon — while giving you visibility and control over how they do it.

🛡️

Moderated communities

Every community your kid joins is monitored, and anyone can be reported or blocked in one tap.

🔗

Linked accounts

Your kid's account is linked to yours with a 4-digit parent code, so you always know who's connected to who.

🚩

Reports go to you

If your kid reports or blocks someone, you'll be able to see it here from your parent dashboard.

🎮

Kid-safe sign in

Your kid signs in with just a username and your code — no email, no password to manage.
